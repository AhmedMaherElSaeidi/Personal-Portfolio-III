# Prompt: Build a Premium Personal Portfolio for Ahmed Maher — Software Engineer

Act as a **senior UI/UX designer, creative frontend engineer, and software architect**. Design and build a complete, production-ready personal portfolio website for **Ahmed Maher, a Software Engineer based in Cairo, Egypt**.

The website should feel like a premium, custom-designed digital product rather than a generic developer portfolio template. It should communicate technical expertise, creativity, professionalism, and attention to detail while remaining intuitive, accessible, fast, and visually comfortable.

The portfolio's primary goal is to help recruiters, hiring managers, and potential clients quickly understand my background, explore my projects, and contact me.

---

# 1. Design Philosophy and Visual Identity

Create a **minimalist, modern, sophisticated, and visually distinctive** design.

The design should combine:

* Premium SaaS product aesthetics.
* Modern editorial layouts and typography.
* Subtle glass effects where appropriate.
* Clean layouts with generous whitespace.
* Carefully chosen accent colors.
* Smooth, purposeful animations.
* A clear visual hierarchy.

### Visual direction

Avoid the typical portfolio design with a huge gradient background, excessive glowing effects, generic floating cards, or unnecessary animations.

Instead, create a visual identity that feels custom-built for a software engineer who works across full-stack development, enterprise software, and AI.

**Suggested color palette:**

Dark theme:

* Background: Deep charcoal or near-black (#101114).
* Secondary surfaces: Slightly lighter charcoal (#191B20).
* Primary text: Soft white (#F3F4F6).
* Secondary text: Muted gray (#A1A1AA).
* Accent: Electric blue, cyan, or a carefully selected blue-violet.
* Borders: Subtle, low-contrast gray.

Light theme:

* Background: Soft off-white (#F8F9FB).
* Secondary surfaces: Pure white (#FFFFFF).
* Primary text: Deep charcoal (#18181B).
* Secondary text: Muted gray (#71717A).
* Accent: A complementary shade of the dark theme's accent.
* Borders: Soft gray with subtle contrast.

Keep the accent color consistent across both themes.

Ensure that colors maintain sufficient contrast and that the website remains comfortable to look at during extended browsing.

### Typography

Use modern, highly readable typography.

Suggested fonts:

* Headings: Inter, Manrope, or Geist.
* Body: Inter or Geist.
* Code snippets and technical labels: JetBrains Mono.

Use a clear type scale, carefully controlled line lengths, and appropriate font weights.

Headings should feel expressive without becoming oversized or overwhelming.

# 11. Animation and Interaction Design

Animations should make the interface feel polished and responsive without distracting visitors.

Use a consistent animation system.

### Recommended animations

* Subtle fade and slide transitions when sections enter the viewport.
* Gentle staggered entrance animations for related elements.
* Smooth navigation scrolling.
* Subtle button hover and press states.
* Small card elevation or border changes on hover.
* Smooth theme switching.
* A polished mobile navigation transition.
* Minimal decorative animation in the hero section.

### Animation rules

* Prioritize performance and responsiveness.
* Avoid excessive parallax effects.
* Avoid continuous motion that draws attention away from content.
* Avoid aggressive scroll hijacking.
* Avoid excessive bounce, rotation, flashing, and large-scale movement.
* Respect the `prefers-reduced-motion` accessibility setting.
* Ensure animations do not interfere with reading, clicking, or keyboard navigation.

Use Framer Motion or Motion where appropriate, but do not introduce a heavy animation dependency just for simple CSS transitions.
